2-minute survey

Where does compute access break down for you?

This survey is for people who could not find a resource that fits their needs. It asks about access, technical support, information, and public benefit. Your answers help us design a more equitable access model for public AI compute.

Research prototype. This is early student research. There is no live CalCompute service and no final eligibility decision. Responses are anonymous unless you choose to add an email. Do not enter sensitive personal or organizational data.

Takes about 2 minutes · 12 scored questions + 5 background questions

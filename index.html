Why this prototype exists

Universal eligibility does not automatically mean equitable access.

Access

Resources are uneven

Some institutions have labs, clusters, and staff. Others have a laptop, a budget, and no one to call.

Capacity

Support is uneven

Knowing how to use AI compute is a skill. Training and technical support are part of access.

Information

Eligibility is opaque

People often do not know which programs they qualify for, or how to apply.
